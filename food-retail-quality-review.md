## Cluster-level findings

### Unique intent

All 5 pages have a defendable separate intent:

- `utylizaciya-harchovyh-produktiv.html`: general umbrella page for food products and mixed food lots.
- `utylizaciya-fruktiv-ta-ovochiv.html`: organic produce stream with spoilage, wet organics, seasonal lots, and packaging separation.
- `utylizaciya-sokiv-ta-napoyiv.html`: liquid food products plus packaging, leakage, mixed beverage groups, and container types.
- `utylizaciya-energetychnyh-napoyiv.html`: narrower beverage category with cans/PET, sticky residues, multipacks, and SKU-like retail lots.
- `spysannya-produktiv.html`: documentary/accounting process before disposal, not a physical waste category.

### Template risk

Template risk is medium for the cluster.

Reasons:

- The first operational H2 set is almost identical across all 5 pages: `Що це`, `Коли потрібно`, `Коли НЕ потрібна утилізація`, `Коли НЕ потрібно`, `Які документи потрібні`, `Ризики`, `Як проходить процес`, `Типові сценарії`, `Коли потрібен ліцензований підрядник`.
- The checklist under `Що перевірити` is identical on all 5 pages.
- CTA blocks are generic on 4 pages and contextual only on the main food page.
- Some sentences repeat across 3-5 pages, especially around inventory, planned cleanup, temporary storage/logistics, documents after transfer, and journal/register recommendations.

Counterbalance:

- Each page has a distinct `Коротка відповідь`.
- Each page has 3 topic-specific supporting sections.
- FAQ questions are not duplicated verbatim and generally match the page intent.
- Internal links are complete and relevant.

### Content completeness

Coverage is generally adequate for indexing:

- what it is: yes
- when needed: yes
- when not needed: yes
- typical scenarios: yes
- risks: yes
- documents: yes
- process: yes
- mistakes: partial, mostly implied through risks and "when not needed"
- practical checklist: yes, but generic
- FAQ: yes

The biggest content gap is not absence of sections, but lack of page-specific practical detail in the repeated checklist, CTA, and some process paragraphs.
